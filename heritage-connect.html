Heritage Connect

Project Description

Heritage Connect is a digital platform that bridges the gap between local artisans and global customers, providing a marketplace to sell and showcase traditional crafts. This platform preserves cultural heritage, promotes sustainable livelihoods, and empowers artisans to reach a broader audience. Heritage Connect is an innovative digital platform aimed at empowering local artisans by providing them with a global marketplace to showcase and sell their traditional crafts. Through this platform, artisans from diverse cultures and regions are given an opportunity to connect with a worldwide customer base, ensuring the preservation of their cultural heritage while fostering sustainable economic growth. This is a group project developed as part of college coursework to promote digital awareness of Indian heritage and culture.
